Privacy Policy for NeighbourLend

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

NeighbourLend collects information you provide directly to us when creating an account, listing gear, requesting a rental, or submitting photo evidence. This includes your name, email address, general neighbourhood location, listing descriptions, and rental transaction records.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to facilitate peer-to-peer rentals, manage pre-authorization deposit holds, verify item condition via photo check-ins, process payouts via Stripe Connect, and prevent fraud.

3. Payment Data & Stripe

Financial details (credit card info and bank account details for payouts) are processed securely by Stripe. NeighbourLend does not store complete credit card or bank account details on our servers.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared between borrowers and lenders strictly as necessary to facilitate pickup, return, and condition verification of rented items.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or account deletion requests, please contact us at support@neighbourlend.co.uk.
